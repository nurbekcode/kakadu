Online English course: from home, on your schedule

An online English course can fully replace commuting to a language centre. What matters is daily speaking practice and a tutor who corrects your mistakes on time. Kakadu English is built exactly for that.

Why learn online

  • No commute: lessons on your phone or computer, anywhere
  • The schedule adapts to you
  • Much cheaper than a language centre
  • An AI tutor never gets tired and never laughs at mistakes

How a lesson works on Kakadu

You start with a 10-minute placement test and Kakadu builds your personal plan. Every day you have a voice conversation with the AI: it asks questions in English, you answer, and it gently corrects mistakes and explains the rule in Uzbek or Russian.

New words from your chats go into your vocabulary automatically and come back for review on time. As your level grows, you move on to 10-minute live chats with learners around the world.

Getting the most from an online course

  • Practise at least 15 minutes every day: consistency beats long sessions
  • Use headphones for more accurate pronunciation feedback
  • Don’t fear mistakes: each one is a new rule learned
  • Review your progress report once a week

FAQ

Are online English courses effective?

Yes, when they include daily speaking practice and error correction. Watching videos alone is not enough, so most of your time on Kakadu goes to voice conversation.

Are the online lessons free?

Kakadu’s free plan includes a few minutes of AI chat a day, the placement test and core lessons. Paid plans add more practice.

What device do I need?

A phone or computer with a browser and headphones. Nothing to install.
