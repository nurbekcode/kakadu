Learn English from zero: a clear plan

Starting English from scratch feels daunting, but with the right order it is very doable. Here is the path from A1 to B1 and what to do at each stage.

Stage 1 (A1): foundations

  • The alphabet, reading rules and key sounds
  • The 300–500 most common words
  • To be, Present Simple, simple questions
  • Saying 5–6 sentences about yourself

Stage 2 (A2): everyday conversation

  • Past Simple to talk about your day
  • Phrases for shops, cafés and transport
  • 1,000–1,500 words
  • A short 10-minute AI chat every day

Stage 3 (B1): freedom

  • Present Perfect, future forms and conditionals
  • Explaining your opinion and giving reasons
  • Understanding English podcasts and videos
  • Talking with live partners

FAQ

How long does it take to learn English from zero?

Cambridge estimates about 350–400 guided learning hours to reach B1. At 30–60 minutes a day that is usually 1–1.5 years.

Can I learn English by myself?

Yes, with a plan, regular practice and something that corrects your mistakes. On Kakadu the AI tutor plays that role.

Should I start with grammar or speaking?

Do both: a little grammar, then speak right away. Rules stick when you use them.
