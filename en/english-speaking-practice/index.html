English speaking practice, every day

Many learners know the grammar but freeze when it’s time to speak. The reason is simple: speaking is a skill, and you only build it by speaking. Kakadu gives you a safe place to speak every day.

Why speaking feels hard

  • Too little practice: a class gives you a minute or two to talk
  • Fear of mistakes and looking silly
  • You know the words but can’t recall them fast
  • No one corrects your pronunciation

The Kakadu speaking programme

Every day you have a voice conversation with the AI on any topic: a café, an airport, a job interview or free talk. It explains what you missed in Uzbek or Russian, corrects mistakes and scores your pronunciation, grammar, fluency and vocabulary at the end.

Once you feel confident, you move on to 10-minute live chats with a partner at your level. It’s an online speaking club, available any time.

Speak sooner

  • Speak out loud for 10–15 minutes a day
  • Use every new word in a sentence right away
  • Record yourself and listen back
  • Aim to be understood, not to be perfect

FAQ

How long does it take to start speaking English?

With 15 minutes of daily practice, most learners notice speaking gets easier within a few weeks. Full fluency depends on your level.

How do I practise without a partner?

On Kakadu the AI tutor is always ready to talk. Later the platform pairs you with real learners.

Does it correct pronunciation?

Yes. After every chat you see a pronunciation score and the words to work on.
